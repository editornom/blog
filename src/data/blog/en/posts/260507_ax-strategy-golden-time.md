<div class="bluf"><strong>[BLUF]</strong><p>The success of AX transformation depends not on the psychological persuasion of members, but on the bold liquidation of old technical debt (Legacy) and the demonstration of overwhelming 'work speed' through Agentic AI. The most powerful change management strategy is to prioritize building modern infrastructure for the core 30% of adopters to provide tangible efficacy, rather than waiting for universal agreement.</p></div>

As the massive wave of AI Transformation (AX) sweeps across universities and corporations, many leaders are agonizing over "how to reduce resistance among members." However, did you know that the standard discourse of "people-centered change management" we've believed in might actually be a poison that consumes the organization's golden time?

In a rapidly changing AI ecosystem, waiting for perfect consensus from every member is essentially choosing obsolescence. According to a McKinsey report, while 90% of organizations have adopted AI, fewer than 20% have succeeded in actual business scaling—a statistic that holds significant implications for us.

![AX Considerations - Teal and silver crystals breaking through old, rusty metal gears.](../../../../../source/posts/AX를_위해_고려해야할_사항들/41699779-0.webp)

When we look deeper into the reality of resistance to AI adoption within organizations, we discover it isn't just a simple fear or rejection of new technology. Rather, the essence is often the extreme workflow fatigue created by the disharmony between AI and legacy systems accumulated over decades.

If extracting and refining data from an outdated <a href="/ko/glossary/what-is-erp" class="glossary-tooltip" data-definition="An Enterprise Resource Planning solution that manages core business processes such as production, logistics, finance, and HR in a single integrated system to efficiently allocate resources.">ERP</a> system is painful, and you only put a fancy AI wrapper on top of it, members will naturally feel exhausted. We must face the fact that 80% of human resistance stems from the physical constraints imposed by inconvenient systems.

> "True change management in the AX era is not about soothing hearts, but about being the first to build the technical foundation that can set human hands free."

Therefore, the first task for university presidents or C-level executives should not be "psychological care" but "resolving technical debt." Breaking down data silos and carrying out ERP modernization is, in fact, the true meaning of people-centered management.

When infrastructure is in place to provide clean, high-quality data so that AI can produce tangible results, members will finally begin to perceive AI not as a "threat to their jobs" but as a "powerful tool that enhances their productivity."

Now is the time to look beyond simple chatbots and consider the transition to **Agentic AI**, which can make judgments and perform tasks autonomously. Agentic AI does not stop at following instructions; it designs autonomous workflows, drastically increasing the speed of decision-making.

![AX Considerations - Liquid mercury flowing over a transparent glass circuit board, representing the speed and flexibility of Agentic AI.](../../../../../source/posts/AX를_위해_고려해야할_사항들/eb5afb2d-1.webp)

In an era where the speed of decision-making equals competitiveness, this technological leap will be a key variable determining an organization's fate. In an environment where AI agents can draft and review without going through complex reporting lines, organizational agility is inevitably maximized.

However, a common mistake leaders make during this process is obsessing over "universal acceptance." Rather than expecting all faculty and staff to change at once, a strategy is needed to concentrate firepower on the "core 30%" who are sensitive to change and high in execution.

The so-called "30% Rule" involves saving the enormous energy required to build total consensus and focusing instead on rapidly creating actual success stories. The overwhelming performance and efficiency shown by this group become the most powerful force that naturally moves the remaining 70%.

The table below illustrates the stark difference between leading organizations that successfully drive AX and those that do not. It's clear that the key lies in infrastructure modernization and the resulting execution speed.

| Metric | General Organizations | AX Leaders (High Performers) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ROI (Return on Investment) | 3.7x | 10.3x (IDC Data) |
| AI Scaling Success Rate | Less than 20% | Over 80% (McKinsey Data) |
| Core Strategy | Focused on psychological persuasion | Infrastructure modernization & Agentic AI adoption |

We often worry only about the risks of attempting change, but we overlook the "Cost of Inaction." The gap between the exponentially evolving speed of technology and the linearly moving receptivity of an organization will only widen over time.

While the cost of modernizing infrastructure and adopting AI agents today might seem expensive, the future price of missing that window will be unimaginably high. We must remember that AX is not a one-time project but a continuous evolution model.

![AX Considerations - Sophisticated 3D art with smooth glass textures and mysterious light, representing flexible structures and vital growth.](../../../../../source/posts/AX를_위해_고려해야할_사항들/b770c420-2.webp)

The role of a leader must now shift from a "counselor" who holds hands and persuades to a "pioneer" who clears technical barriers and opens the fastest path. Bear in mind that change management without resolving technical legacy is essentially a waste of expensive resources.

> "The golden time for AI transformation does not wait. Resolving technical debt to provide immediate work efficacy to members—that is the beginning of the most innovative change management."

In conclusion, the success formula for AX is clear: boldly cut out old systems, install the powerful engine of Agentic AI, and prove the difference in speed through the core 30% of your workforce. This is the only path that will transform your organization into a true AI leader.
