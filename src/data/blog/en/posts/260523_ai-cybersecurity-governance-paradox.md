 ## 1. Fundamental Changes in AI Cybersecurity Identified by F5 CEO

 ### 1.1. Accelerated Threats: Traditional Security Failing to Keep Up

 The frontlines of cybersecurity are being reshaped at an unprecedented pace. The CEO of F5 recently issued a strong warning that the tempo of AI-driven attacks has already exceeded the physical limits that traditional defense systems can handle.

 Simply raising firewalls is no longer enough to stop the massive tide. Attackers are using Generative AI to automate everything from vulnerability scanning to the actual execution of attacks, pressuring organizations with near real-time velocity.

 Traditional security processes tend to consume too much time in decision-making and verification. In reality, these delays provide opportunities for attackers and leave fatal gaps for defenders.

 ![AI Cybersecurity - An abstract image of a digital clock shattering under the pressure of data moving at the speed of light.](../../../../../source/posts/AI_사이버_보안/8b4735f0-0.webp)

 ### 1.2. Redefining Technical Superiority: Speed is Security

 The core of modern cybersecurity is shifting from static defensive strength to dynamic response speed. Gaining a technical edge no longer means just having more complex algorithms; it means securing the tempo required to neutralize threats the moment they arise.

 A critical concept here is <a href="/en/glossary/ai-governance" class="glossary-tooltip" data-definition="An organizational framework designed to ensure the ethical use, safety, transparency, and regulatory compliance of AI systems.">AI Governance</a>. If an organization's management framework cannot support the speed of innovation, that governance effectively becomes a security vulnerability.

 Security leaders must now prioritize the value of being 'fast and flexible' over being 'safe and slow.' True defensive power is achieved only when the new definition—that speed is security—is fully embraced.

 ## 2. Anthropic vs. OpenAI: The Governance Paradox and Market Reality

 ### 2.1. Gary Marcus's Assessment of Anthropic's 'Stability' and its Flip Side

 NYU Professor Gary Marcus has often evaluated Anthropic's models as having higher reliability and stability compared to OpenAI. This is a result of a corporate philosophy that prioritizes governance.

 However, one must not overlook that this 'relative stability' can become a poison in a rapidly changing security market. The numerous validation steps required to ensure stability often cause delays in real-world response scenarios.

 The case of Anthropic poses a vital question: are we missing out on disruptive innovation and the necessary response tempo by pursuing perfect governance?

 ### 2.2. How Stable Governance Hinders Disruptive Innovation

 As governance becomes more rigid, organizations tend to make conservative choices, leading to hesitation in adopting disruptive technologies. This excessive proceduralism yields catastrophic results, especially during a barrage of unexpected <a href="/en/glossary/zero-day-exploit" class="glossary-tooltip" data-definition="A type of cyber attack that occurs after a security vulnerability is discovered but before a patch has been applied.">zero-day exploits</a>.

 > When the shield of 'governance stability' becomes a shackle on innovation, security has already failed. Modern security is not a static defense but dynamic acceleration. Any governance that fails to outpace the attacker's tempo is merely a product of complacency.

 Ultimately, governance must not be a tool to control innovation but an accelerator that helps it move faster in the right direction. To achieve this, rigid management models must be completely dismantled and reconstructed.

 ## 3. Conclusion: Reject 'Safe Obsolescence'

 ### 3.1. Complacency Hidden in Governance: A Warning to Security Professionals

 Many security professionals must ask themselves if they are choosing 'safe obsolescence' by hiding behind stable governance systems. Feeling a sense of relief just because regulations are being followed is an attitude that attackers welcome most.

 As the F5 CEO diagnosed, AI-based attacks are accelerating beyond physical limits. In this climate, governance relying on yesterday's manuals provides no protection and instead paralyzes actual response capabilities.

 We must wake up from the illusion of stability and tear down every barrier hindering technological innovation. Governance without practical threat response capability is nothing more than a beautifully packaged record of failure.

 ### 3.2. Future Survival Strategy: Moving Toward Disruptive Security Innovation

 Survival in the future depends not on simply maintaining systems, but on staying a step ahead of attackers through technological leaps. Here are the specific shifts that security leaders should focus on:

 * F5 CEO's Diagnosis: AI-driven attacks accelerate the tempo of cybersecurity beyond the point of physical human response.
 * Gary Marcus (NYU Professor) Metric: Anthropic's 'relative stability' may lead to fatal innovation delays in a volatile security market.
 * Macro Variables (e.g., Kevin Warsh Fed Era): A reform-oriented financial environment will force tech companies to invest in aggressive security innovation rather than settle for stability.
