---# What is AGI?

### Dictionary Definition
Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) refers to machines with the intelligence to perform any intellectual task that a human can do at an equivalent or superior level. It represents a move beyond 'Narrow AI'—which is designed to solve tasks only in specific domains—to a level where a machine can autonomously learn, exercise judgment, and solve complex problems across diverse situations. In the modern AI industry, AGI is regarded as the ultimate technical milestone to be achieved by expanding the parameters and data volume of Large Language Models (LLMs).

### Practical Use Case
- **Setting AI Technology Roadmaps**: AGI serves as the target for R&D and investment strategies aimed at securing generalized intelligence through 'Scaling Laws,' which involve maximizing model size and computational power.
- **Evaluating Technical Tipping Points**: It is used as a benchmark to assess whether current AI architectures have entered a phase of 'diminishing returns' in terms of scalability or are successfully evolving into true general intelligence.
