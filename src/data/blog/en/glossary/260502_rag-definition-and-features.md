# What is Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)?

### Dictionary Definition
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is a technical framework that enables Large Language Models (LLM) to go beyond their pre-trained knowledge by retrieving relevant information from external knowledge bases and integrating it into the generation process. This technology allows models to reflect up-to-date information or leverage specialized domain knowledge. Its primary goals are to suppress hallucinations—where models present incorrect information as fact—and to ensure that responses are grounded in verifiable evidence.

### Practical Use Case
RAG is used to build knowledge management systems that connect LLMs to internal corporate databases containing security regulations or manuals, allowing employees to receive answers generated in real-time based on those documents. It is also explored as a practical solution for AI security auditing, where it helps control the non-deterministic nature of models and manages permission-based information disclosure through Knowledge Layer analysis.
