# What is ERP?

### Dictionary Definition
Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) is a solution that integrates and manages core business processes—including finance, accounting, production, logistics, and human resources—into a single, unified system. It refers to an integrated information infrastructure that consolidates data scattered across an organization into a single database for real-time sharing. This prevents redundant tasks between departments and maximizes the efficiency of enterprise-wide resource utilization.

### Practical Use Case
When pursuing an AI Transformation (AX) strategy, organizations modernize aging legacy ERP systems that often hinder data refinement and flow to resolve technical debt. An advanced ERP serves as the technical foundation, providing the high-quality data necessary for AI agents to perform tasks autonomously. Through this modernization, companies can significantly enhance their organizational agility and the speed of strategic decision-making.
