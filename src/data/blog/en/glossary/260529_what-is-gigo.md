# What is GIGO?

### Dictionary Definition
GIGO stands for 'Garbage In, Garbage Out.' it is a foundational maxim in computer science and information technology stating that if poor or nonsensical data is input into a system, the resulting output will be equally poor or nonsensical. This principle emphasizes that the quality of the output is determined not only by the processing logic but primarily by the quality of the foundational input data.

### Practical Use Case
In the Artificial Intelligence (AI) industry, even when utilizing sophisticated algorithms such as the Transformer architecture, model performance will degrade if the training data is not properly refined. Particularly in the commercialization of Large Language Models (LLMs), it has been observed that advanced models are increasingly sensitive to low-quality data, leading to unreliable results—a phenomenon referred to as the 'evolution of GIGO.' Consequently, the importance of data governance is being highlighted as a critical solution to maximize the efficacy of these models.

### Related Words
- **Data Governance**: A management framework used to maintain the quality, consistency, and security of data.
- **Transformer**: A core algorithm architecture in modern AI, optimized for parallel processing and context-aware learning.
- **Data Context**: Refers to the contextual maturity and inherent value that data possesses within a specific environment.
