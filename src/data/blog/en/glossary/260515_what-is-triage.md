### Dictionary Definition
The term "Triage" originally stems from the medical field, where it refers to the practice of determining the priority of patients' treatments based on the severity and urgency of their condition. In the context of IT and cybersecurity, it refers to the process of analyzing a vast number of reported bugs, security vulnerabilities, or system issues and assigning processing priority based on their severity and potential business impact. This is a strategic decision-making procedure designed to concentrate limited resources and time on resolving the most critical issues first.

### Practical Use Case
Security Operations (SecOps) teams perform triage on a massive volume of security alerts generated by AI-driven detection tools. During this process, they identify key vulnerabilities that are highly likely to lead to an actual attack for immediate patching. Conversely, they filter out 'false positive' reports that have a low probability of execution or negligible impact, thereby significantly improving overall operational efficiency.

### Related Words
* **Reporting DoS**: A phenomenon where the security team's triage (priority analysis) function is paralyzed due to a massive influx of AI-generated reports.
* **Vulnerability Management**: A continuous lifecycle process of identifying, evaluating, and remediating security weaknesses.
* **Severity**: A metric that represents the extent of the technical impact a vulnerability has on a system.
