# What is METR?

### Dictionary Definition
METR (Model Evaluation and Threat Research) is an elite nonprofit research institute focused on evaluating the capabilities and structural risks of frontier AI models. It designs rigorous test suites to measure AI autonomy, potential misuse in biological/chemical contexts, and offensive cybersecurity capabilities. Originally born as the evaluation branch ('ARC Evals') of the Alignment Research Center (ARC), it now operates as an independent global standard for pre-deployment AI safety testing.

### Practical Use Case
Global tech leaders such as Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic submit early versions of their foundation models to METR for restricted-access risk evaluations. In a secure sandbox, METR tasks the model with complex, long-horizon objectives (e.g., "autonomously find and exploit a flaw on a target server"). METR then measures the model's **Time Horizon**—the length of time the model can operate entirely autonomously without looping, crashing, or requiring human correction.
