# What is EUV (Extreme Ultraviolet)?

### Technical Definition
EUV (Extreme Ultraviolet) is a light source with a wavelength of 13.5nm used in the semiconductor lithography process to print circuit patterns onto silicon wafers. Its wavelength is approximately 1/14th the length of the Argon Fluoride (ArF) light sources used in conventional processes, making it an essential next-generation technology for implementing ultra-fine circuit patterns of 7 nanometers (nm) or less.

### Practical Use Case
Global foundry companies are deploying EUV lithography equipment for the mass production of ultra-fine semiconductors, such as 5nm and 2nm processes. By narrowing circuit line widths, they increase chip density and produce high-performance processors that maximize both power efficiency and computational power.
