# What is LLMOps?

## Dictionary Definition
LLMOps (Large Language Model Operations) is an operational framework designed to systematize and automate the development, deployment, maintenance, and monitoring processes of Large Language Models (LLMs). It extends the core concepts of MLOps to address the specific needs of LLMs, managing the entire lifecycle including model fine-tuning, prompt engineering, data governance, and the implementation of real-time security guardrails. Its primary goal is to enhance the productivity of AI technology in corporate environments while optimizing security risks and management costs.

## Practical Use Case
When an organization adopts Generative AI, LLMOps is utilized as an operational system to establish a standardized internal AI platform. It continuously verifies compliance with security regulations through real-time automated monitoring systems, effectively preventing data leaks (Shadow AI) caused by employees using unauthorized external tools.

## Related Words
- MLOps: A framework for integrating and managing the development and operation of machine learning models.
- Generative AI Governance: A policy-driven management framework designed to ensure transparency and safety in the use of AI within an organization.
- Shadow AI: AI services introduced and used by employees without official authorization from the IT department.
