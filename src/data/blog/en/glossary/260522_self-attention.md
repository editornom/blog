# What is Self-Attention?

Self-attention is a mechanism that allows an AI model to quantify the relational importance of all other words in a sentence when processing a specific word. This process effectively captures contextual information and plays a pivotal role in solving the 'long-term dependency' problem inherent in traditional sequential processing methods.

### Practical Use Cases
The self-attention mechanism is the fundamental component of Google's Transformer architecture and is widely utilized in Large Language Models (LLMs) such as ChatGPT and Google Gemini. It enables high-level contextual understanding, delivering superior performance in complex Natural Language Processing (NLP) tasks including machine translation, text summarization, and question-answering systems.
