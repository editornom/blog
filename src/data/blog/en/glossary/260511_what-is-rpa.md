### Dictionary Definition
Robotic Process Automation (RPA) is a technology that uses software robots to automate structured and repetitive business processes typically performed by humans on a computer. It focuses on processing structured data according to predefined rules (rule-based) rather than intelligent judgment, with the primary goals of increasing task accuracy and reducing operational costs.

### Practical Use Case
In financial institutions, RPA is widely used to input large volumes of loan application documents into specific databases. HR departments utilize it to automate monthly recurring tasks such as payroll calculations and the issuance of payslips. Additionally, logistics companies implement RPA for simple, repetitive tasks like entering tracking numbers and updating inventory status to enhance operational efficiency.
