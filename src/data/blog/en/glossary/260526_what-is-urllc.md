# What is URLLC?

### Dictionary Definition
URLLC (Ultra-Reliable Low-Latency Communications) is one of the three core service categories of 5G mobile communications. It is a technical specification that guarantees extremely low latency and exceptionally high data transmission reliability. Typically aiming for a latency of 1ms or less and a transmission success rate of over 99.999%, it serves as a foundational technology for ensuring the stability of mission-critical industrial services where real-time processing is indispensable.

### Practical Use Case
URLLC is utilized in precision robot control systems within smart factories and for establishing V2X (Vehicle-to-Everything) communication environments for autonomous vehicles. It is also adopted in high-risk business sectors where even a one-second delay or a connection error could lead to catastrophic accidents, such as remote surgery or drone control. To fully guarantee URLLC performance in actual operating environments, end-to-end resource isolation through Network Slicing based on 5G SA (Standalone) infrastructure is required.

### Related Terms
5G SA (Standalone), Network Slicing, eMBB (Enhanced Mobile Broadband)
