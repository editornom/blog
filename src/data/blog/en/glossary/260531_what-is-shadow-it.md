## What is Shadow IT?

### Dictionary Definition
Shadow IT refers to information technology systems, software, applications, and services used within an organization without the official approval or management of the IT department. This phenomenon primarily occurs when individual departments or employees attempt to bypass complex approval processes to immediately improve work efficiency, and it has become increasingly prevalent with the widespread adoption of Cloud computing and SaaS. Since these assets exist outside the organization's central control, they are a major cause of governance issues, including exposure to security vulnerabilities, data breach risks, and lack of transparency in cost management.

### Practical Use Case
A common scenario occurs when a company's Cloud governance automation policies are excessively strict, causing significant delays in resource provisioning and deployment. To maintain productivity, a development team might bypass official procedures and use a personal credit card to spin up an external public Cloud instance to move forward with their project.
