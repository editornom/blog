### Dictionary Definition
Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is a search technology focused on optimizing specific content to be selected as the primary source when AI-powered answer engines provide direct solutions to user queries. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) refers to a comprehensive strategy aimed at enhancing the credibility, citability, and structural clarity of content to align with the algorithms of generative AI systems, such as Large Language Models (LLMs), as they aggregate information and generate responses. This represents an evolution from traditional Search Engine Optimization (SEO), which targets link exposure on Search Engine Results Pages (SERPs), toward a focus on ensuring information is integrated directly into the final sentences generated by AI.

### Practical Use Case
- Apply structured data (Schema Markup) to websites to help search engines accurately grasp the context of the data, encouraging AI chatbots to cite specific product information correctly.
- Provide key information alongside summarized lists or statistical figures to make it easier for AI to answer specific questions, thereby increasing exposure frequency within "answer engines."
