## What is RLHF?

### Dictionary Definition
RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) is a technical methodology used to align AI model outputs with human values, intentions, and preferences by utilizing human feedback as a reward signal within a reinforcement learning framework. It is designed to ensure that Large Language Models (LLMs) go beyond simply predicting the next token based on statistical distributions; instead, they learn to produce responses that meet subjective human standards for quality and social norms.

### Practical Use Case
In practice, human reviewers rank multiple candidate responses generated by a language model based on their preference. This data is used to train a Reward Model, which quantifies the level of satisfaction a human would derive from a specific response. Finally, the model is optimized using the PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization) algorithm to generate responses that maximize these reward scores. During this process, KL Divergence (Kullback-Leibler Divergence) is applied to limit deviations from the initial model, ensuring that the AI maintains its original linguistic consistency while improving its alignment.
